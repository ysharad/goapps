Account & Data Deletion

Locus

Delete your Locus account and data · Package com.goapps.locus
Provided by Mags Apps (Sharad Yadav) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short
  • In version 1.0.1 and later, you can erase everything and delete your account from inside the app. You need an internet connection.
  • On an earlier version, in-app deletion was incomplete. Update, or clear Locus’s storage in Android settings and email us; we'll delete the online copy within 30 days.
  • Your chats live on your phone. Deleting the app's data removes them permanently — we can't recover them, because we never had the keys.

Option 1 — Delete everything from inside the app

On version 1.0.1 and later this removes what is on your phone and what is stored online for your account.

  1. Open Locus.
  2. Go to the You tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Export or delete my data.
  5. Tap Delete everything and confirm.

Locus closes, and starts from the first-run screen next time. This cannot be undone. Without a connection you can erase only the phone, then use Option 2 for the online copy.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete your account

If you are on an earlier version, erased only your phone, or no longer have the app installed:

  • Email hello@goapps.store with the subject “Delete my Locus account”.
  • Send it from the email address you signed in with, or tell us the phone number you used, so we can identify the account. If you never logged in, include the key shown on your You tab, if you still have it.
  • We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

For “Delete everything” in version 1.0.1 and later:

DataWhat happens
Your profile (name, age, bio, interests, glyph)Deleted from your phone and from our servers
Your connections, swipe history, chats and keysDeleted from your phone
Your public chat key and notification tokenDeleted from our servers
Encrypted messages waiting for you in the relayDeleted. Messages you already sent stay until the recipient receives them
Your Locus sign-in account (anonymous, Google or phone)Deleted. Your Google account itself is not affected
Safety reports made by you or about youKept for up to 12 months so we can act on abuse, then deleted. These contain the report reason, the identifiers involved and the reported profile, not your chats
Analytics and advertising data held by GoogleKept under Google’s retention settings and policies. Locus’s app-instance identifier is reset on your phone

Copies may persist briefly in encrypted backups after deletion and are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

A note about other people's phones

Locus works device to device. If you chatted with someone, their copy of your messages is on their phone, and deleting your account does not remove it — just as deleting a text message does not remove it from the person you sent it to.
