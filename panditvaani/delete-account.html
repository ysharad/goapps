App: PanditVaani (package com.goapps.astrovaani) · Publisher: GoApps

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@goapps.store with the subject "PanditVaani account deletion".
  2. Include the name you entered in the app and, if you linked one, the Google email or phone number you used. This lets us find your account.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete everything within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account identifier (anonymous, Google or phone link).
  • Your birth details and computed kundli.
  • All conversations with Pandit ji.

What is kept

Nothing tied to you. Aggregated, anonymous analytics (app opens, crash counts) contain no personal data and cannot be linked back to you.

You can also simply uninstall the app: this removes all local data on your phone immediately, and any server data is deleted on request as above.
