On The Go

The hands-free driving assistant

● In testing

Keep your eyes on the road and talk to your phone, not at it. On The Go turns your voice into action — reply to messages, hear who called, open apps, and navigate, all without touching the screen.

  • 🎙️Voice commands — reply on WhatsApp, call back, open any app, or start navigation just by speaking.
  • ✨“Hey OG” wake word — trigger it completely hands-free, no tapping required.
  • 🚙Drive Mode — starts automatically when your car connects over Bluetooth, and stops when you’re done.
  • 💬Messages read aloud — hear new WhatsApp and SMS messages and reply without reaching for the phone.
  • 📞Never miss a call — “Who called?” and call them back with a word.
  • 🔒Private by design — works on-device; nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on smart understanding.

Locus

Meet the people around you

● Coming soon

Locus helps you discover and connect with the people right around you — over a private Bluetooth mesh that works even without internet. See who’s nearby, connect when the interest is mutual, and let a little AI help you break the ice.

  • 📡Nearby discovery — find people around you over an offline Bluetooth mesh, no signal required.
  • 💜Mutual connections — you only connect when the interest goes both ways.
  • ✨AI icebreakers — never face a blank chat; get smart, tailored openers for every match.
  • 🛡️Private & offline-first — discovery happens device-to-device, and you stay anonymous by default.
