Before you install

Is this a dating app?
No. It's for meeting the people already around you, whatever you're there for. Your card says which.
Does it drain the battery?
No. It sits quiet when the room is quiet. A long night is fine.
What happens when I leave?
People drop off your radar as they go out of range. Chats you've already started keep working.
Can someone track me with this?
Your card is visible to people near you while Locus is on. That's the point, and it's the only thing that is. No location, no history, and you can go dark instantly.
